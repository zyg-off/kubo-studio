Billing questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
From your customer portal: enter the e-mail you used to buy, then choose "Cancel". Your access stays active until the end of the period already paid.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. You have 14 days after subscribing to change your mind and get a refund, with no reason needed. Just write to us.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Bank cards, and the other methods offered on the payment page. Payment is handled by Polar: we never see your card details.
Where can I find my invoices?
They're e-mailed after each payment, and available in your customer portal.
Why is the seller called Polar?
Polar is our authorised reseller: it takes the payment, issues the invoice and handles taxes for us. That's why its name appears on your bank statement.
