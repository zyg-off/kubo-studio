Frequently asked questions

Is Pécule really free?
Yes. The free version has no ads and no time limit. Pécule Pro is an optional subscription for advanced features.
Is Pécule made for France?
Budgets, analytics, goals and household features work wherever you live. Some tools are designed for France: French savings accounts, tax return help and benefit simulators.
Does it work on Mac?
Not yet: Pécule runs on Windows 10 and 11. On your phone, a web app gives you access to your accounts and household.
Windows shows a warning during installation
That's normal: the installer isn't digitally signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Installation takes 2 to 4 minutes.
