A problem with your account?

I don't receive the sign-in code
Check that you're using the e-mail address entered when you bought, and look in your spam folder. If nothing arrives after a few minutes, write to us.
I've lost my Pécule Pro key
It's in the e-mail you received after buying, and in your subscriptions. One key works on up to 3 computers.
My MonKiosk plan hasn't turned on
In MonKiosk, click "J'ai payé : actualiser" (I've paid: refresh). Activation usually takes a few seconds. If your shop stays locked, write to us with your order reference.
I want to change my subscription e-mail
Write to us from the old address with the new one: we'll take care of it.
How do I get a refund?
You have 14 days after subscribing to change your mind, with no reason needed. Write to us: the refund arrives within 14 days. The details are in our terms of sale (in French).
