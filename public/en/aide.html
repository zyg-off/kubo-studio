Subscriptions and payment

How do I cancel my subscription?
From your customer portal: enter the e-mail you used to buy, receive a sign-in code, then choose "Cancel". Your access stays active until the end of the period already paid, and no further payment is taken.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. You have 14 days after subscribing to change your mind and get a refund, with no reason needed. Just write to us. The details are in our terms of sale (in French).
Is payment secure?
Yes. Payments are handled by Polar, a platform specialised in selling software, which acts as our authorised reseller. Kubo Studio never sees your card details.
Where can I find my invoices?
They're e-mailed after each payment, and stay available in your customer portal.

MonKiosk

How does the free trial work?
Create your shop in MonKiosk, then choose your plan: the first 14 days are free. The first payment only happens at the end of the trial, and you can cancel before if the app isn't right for you.
How is the per-employee price calculated?
MonKiosk counts the employees registered in your team. Each month, the highest number reached that month is used. For example, with the Pro plan and 4 employees: €29 + 4 × €2.50 = €39 a month.
Can I change plans?
Yes, whenever you like, from MonKiosk under Compte › Mon abonnement (Account › My subscription).
Which devices does MonKiosk work on?
Any browser, your phone (by adding the page to the home screen) and the Windows app, which updates itself. Data is synced live across all your devices.
Can my team use MonKiosk?
Yes: a subscription covers one shop and its whole team. With the Pro and Premium plans, each employee can have their own access to see their schedule, request leave or clock in.

Pécule

Is Pécule really free?
Yes, with no ads and no time limit. Pécule Pro is an optional subscription for advanced features.
How do I activate Pécule Pro?
After subscribing, you receive a licence key by e-mail. In Pécule, go to Settings › ⭐ Pécule Pro and click "I have a key". One key works on up to 3 computers.
Windows shows a warning during installation
That's normal: the installer isn't digitally signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
Does it work on Mac?
Not yet: Pécule runs on Windows 10 and 11.

Your data

Where is my data stored?
For Pécule, on your computer. For MonKiosk, online, so it can be synced across your devices. All the details are in our privacy policy (in French).
