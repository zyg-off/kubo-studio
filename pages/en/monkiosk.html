Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?
Create your shop in MonKiosk, then choose your plan: the first 14 days are free. The first payment only happens at the end of the trial, and you can cancel before if the app isn't right for you.
How is the per-employee price calculated?
MonKiosk counts the employees registered in your team. Each month, the highest number reached that month is used. For example, with the Pro plan and 4 employees: €29 + 4 × €2.50 = €39 a month.
Can I change plans?
Yes, whenever you like, from MonKiosk under Compte › Mon abonnement (Account › My subscription).
Can I try it without signing up?
Yes. The online demo opens a fictional bakery with its team and schedule. Nothing is saved.
Which devices does it work on?
Any browser, your phone (by adding it to the home screen) and the Windows app. Everything is synced live.
Which country is MonKiosk designed for?
MonKiosk is designed for shops in France: its legal alerts follow French labour rules. Contact us if you're in another country.
